Rule used: no weld is made inside a closed cell and no slot welds are used. Internal joints are double fillets made while the pockets are open; the outer end plate is PPBW where reached through the open side bays and fitted for bearing elsewhere; the side plates close the box and are PPBW to both end plates from outside. Sequence for discussion with the fabricator; the final order follows their welding procedure and access check. FW = fillet weld (leg s), BS = both sides, PPBW = partial penetration butt weld. The model is simplified: corner snipes 10x10 and bar holes are not drawn, and one-side welds are drawn on the face the welder works from.
